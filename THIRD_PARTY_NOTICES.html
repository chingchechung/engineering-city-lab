A free machine-readable copy of the entire geographic database used by this city is js/nyc-geodata.js (the exported GEO object, with original source header). Its database version is 1, in metres around Fifth Avenue and 42nd Street. The exact original inputs and transformation scripts are also available: polygon simplification, projection to the city frame, clipping and derived street/park geometry. No geographic data was changed for the educational integration.
